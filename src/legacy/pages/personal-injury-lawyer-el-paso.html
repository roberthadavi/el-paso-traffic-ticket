Understanding Texas Personal Injury Laws

The Statute of Limitations

In Texas, you generally have two years from the date of the accident to file a personal injury lawsuit. Waiting too long can permanently bar you from seeking justice.

Modified Comparative Negligence

Texas follows the “51% Rule.” This means you can still recover damages even if you were partially at fault for the accident, as long as your responsibility is 50% or less. However, your compensation will be reduced by your percentage of fault.

$$text{Recovery Amount} = text{Total Damages} times (100% – text{Your % of Fault})$$

For example, if a jury awards you $100,000 but finds you 20% at fault, you would receive $80,000.

Should I talk to the insurance company after an accident?

No. Insurance adjusters are trained to minimize payouts. They may ask for a “recorded statement” to use against you later. Always speak with your attorney before giving any statements to the other party’s insurance.

How long will my case take?

A typical case can take anywhere from a few months to over a year, depending on the complexity of the medical treatment and whether the insurance company offers a fair settlement or insists on going to trial.
