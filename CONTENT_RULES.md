## Hard rules (validator enforces most of these)
1. **No Texas "points".** The Driver Responsibility Program (points + surcharges) was repealed effective Sept 1, 2019. Never say Texas gives 2/3 points, never mention DRP surcharges as current. If the topic needs it, use: *"Texas no longer uses the former Driver Responsibility Program points system. Repeated moving-violation convictions can still affect a driver's record and may lead to license suspension in some circumstances."* Historical mention only where clearly labeled repealed.
2. **No guarantees or statistics.** Banned: "always", "will be dismissed", "we can get it dismissed", "guaranteed", "in nearly every case", "most cases"/"in most cases", "high dismissal rate", "usually recalled within 24–48 hours", any `%` figure, any dismissal/outcome/insurance-increase number, "typically within X hours/days". Replace with qualified language: *"Our goal is to pursue the best available outcome based on the citation, driving record, court and circumstances."* / *"A traffic conviction may affect insurance premiums depending on the driver's history, carrier and other rating factors."* Timing: *"Warrant recall timing depends on the court's docket; many El Paso courts act within days once an attorney files, but no timeline is guaranteed."*
3. **No invented facts.** Court procedures, fines, fees, addresses, phone numbers, websites, statutes only when verified (court pages: cite the official source in the JSON `sources` array). Court costs/fines: say they are set by the court. Attorney fees: keep exactly the existing prices.
4. **Do not change prices, slugs, URLs, `related`, `next`, image placeholders or the JSON schema.** You may edit `title`, `metaDescription`, `h1`, `intro`, `summary`, `summaryHeading`, `sections`, `faqs`, and add `published`/`updated`.
5. **Filler budget:** the following may appear at most once across the whole site, so just don't use them: "navigate the legal process", "peace of mind", "we understand how stressful", "experienced representation", "dedicated legal team", "protect your rights", "fight for the best possible outcome", "every case is unique", "don't face this alone", "fight for you", "aggressive representation", "personal touch", "path to recovery", "click here".
6. **Not the only focus.** The firm is *known for* traffic-ticket and warrant defense and Attorney Robert Navar *also assists with* expungement, estate planning, occupational licenses and injury claims. Never "only focus"/"sole focus".
7. **Texas facts you may rely on:** paying a Class C citation enters a plea and results in a conviction on the driving record; deferred disposition and the driving safety course (defensive driving) are discretionary options under Tex. Code Crim. Proc. art. 45.051 / 45.0511 with eligibility limits (e.g. CDL holders, 25+ mph over in some courts, no valid insurance); failure to appear can lead to a warrant and a separate Violate Promise to Appear charge; OMNI/Failure to Appear program holds block DPS license renewal until the court clears them; DWLI is Tex. Transp. Code §521.457; occupational licenses are Tex. Transp. Code ch. 521 subch. L; expunction is Tex. Code Crim. Proc. ch. 55; nondisclosure is Tex. Gov't Code ch. 411 subch. E-1. Qualify everything ("in general", "depending on the court").
